## Design bar — "LACQUER POP" (fresh visual language, per the Design Variety Law)
- **NEVER use the ivory-paper + near-black-ink + single-accent house template.** This site must look nothing like a generic agency template.
- **Palette:** deep aubergine plum (near-black plum) base, hot coral-red and vivid pink as the two accents, warm off-white type. Colour-first, like a wall of nail polish.
- **Typography:** chunky rounded grotesque display type, huge and tight, uppercase for headlines; clean readable sans for body. Big numerals, big CTAs.
- **Layout idiom:** nail-polish swatch chips as the recurring motif — services as a wall of colour swatches, gallery as a full-bleed swatch grid, booking CTA as a giant coral colour block.
- **Motion personality:** playful and kinetic — swatches pop and slide in with stagger, a marquee strip of service names, colour fills on hover. One hero move per moment; payoff stillness after.
- **Hero moment:** giant "QUEEN NAIL" type over a full-bleed nail-art visual, with a coral "Book now" block. Colour first, booking second.
- **Psychology first:** a first-time visitor in Jurong East must understand in 3 seconds — what it is (nail salon), where (Jurong East Ave 1), and how to book (one tap). Booking CTA visible on every page; phone number always one tap away.
